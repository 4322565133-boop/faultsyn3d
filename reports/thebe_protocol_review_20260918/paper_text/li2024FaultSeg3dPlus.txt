                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  CONCLUSION
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           Our experiments show the importance and necessity of studying
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        the potential factors that affect CNN-based fault detection, and
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        we find an ideal metric, the BCD, for the fault detection task. From
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        the results on synthetic and field validation data sets, we draw the
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        following conclusions:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             1) The training data set determines the upper limit of the CNN
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                model. By modifying the data generation workflow and
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                enriching the data sampling rate, the model’s generalizability
                                                           DOI:10.1190/geo2022-0778.1




                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                to the field seismic data is significantly improved.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             2) The Dice combing BCE loss function is more suitable than
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                the commonly used cross-entropy loss function for fault
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                segmentation tasks.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             4) Addressing CNN’s transformation invariance limitation, we

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                for more stable fault detection.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           Our findings, based on the U-net and our data set, might have
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        limitations but are generally extendable to similar CNN models
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        due to U-net’s common use in segmentation. Thus, the conclusions
